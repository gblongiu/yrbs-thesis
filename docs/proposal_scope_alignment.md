# Proposal Scope Alignment

The final repository stays aligned with the January 22, 2026 proposal in the areas that matter for the finished thesis:

- The primary question remained fixed on whether `QN24` and `QN25` improve prediction of `QN26` beyond the locked demographic baseline.
- The project remained predictive and non-causal, with weighted estimates used only for descriptive context.
- The retained deliverables still cover the modeling table, data dictionary, EDA outputs, baseline-versus-augmented comparison, calibration review, subgroup robustness, presentation materials, and reproducible submission packaging.

The final repository is narrower than some early planning language in two deliberate ways:

- Secondary outcomes remain in the modeling table for continuity, but they were not expanded into new headline analyses.
- The boosted-model interpretability record is represented by retained permutation-importance outputs rather than a late-stage addition of new explainer tooling.

The repository does not expand beyond the proposal boundary. It does not add new datasets, new outcomes, causal claims, deployment features, or product layers.
